# Overview:
<br>

**The Gods**, whatever they truly are, sleeping, dead, removed, malevolent or altruistic, are seen with a multitude of interpretations, prioritisation and reverence depending on culture, social standing or even momentary needs.

The vast majority of deities are neither purely "good", nor "evil". The only expetions to this might be [[3. Gods & Religion/4. The Nine/D_The Bound One]]

The Most common interpretations in the present day are the *"Hierarchy-Model"* and the *"Aspect-Model"*.

---
Hierarchy-Model:
- All Gods exist as individuals
- Mayor Gods rule over Minor Gods
- Minor Gods take responsibility over parts of the domain of their superiors

Aspect-Model:
- Only [[3. Gods & Religion/2. The One True God/1. The One\|The One]] exists as an individual
- All other "gods" are mere aspects, no individuals themselfes
- Some, most prominently The imperial [[4. The Races/6. The Urashalyr/index\|Urashalyr]], interpret [[3. Gods & Religion/3. The Trinity/1. The Trinity\|The Trinity]] as the truly existing triage of Gods where other proponents view them as primary aspects of The One

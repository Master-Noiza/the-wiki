# Treysh'neth'ar - The Trinity

---

> [!bug|clean no-i ttl-c txt-c] ## Morning and Nightly Prayer to the Trinity
> 
> *"One hand to Shape, the Stars, the Earth and sea,<br>                             
> One breath to bind, what must forever be,<br>                                            
> One flame to set the world alight,<br>                                                                   
> In endless cycles, of day and night.*<br>                                                            
>
> *Three faces, veiled and ever near,<br>                                                                   
> Three Voices, whispers none can hear,<br>     
> Three shadows cast by one decree,<br>    
> Create, preserve, destroy all free.*<br>    
>
> *O Maker of the heavens wide,<br>    
> O Keeper where all fates abide,<br>    
> O Flame that burns what none may mend,<br>    
> To you, we turn, to break or bend."*

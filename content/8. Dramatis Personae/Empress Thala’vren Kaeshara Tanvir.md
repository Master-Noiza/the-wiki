*"I am the fist, draped in the Preserver's shining steel,  
A shield to our people, a bulwark against harm.*

*I am the hammer of the Destroyer, poised for our foes,  
Striking with the strength of a legion's might.*

*I am the hand of the Creator, sowing growth and abundance,  
Guiding prosperity to flourish within our realm."*
